Add a photo folder

A shared folder, or a folder inside one, mounted read-only: Immich shows its photos and videos but never changes, moves or deletes them. DSM's own thumbnails (@eaDir), the recycle bin and snapshots are skipped. Up to five folders. Synology Photos keeps each person's own photos in /volume1/homes/<name>/Photos.

Uploads

Phones and the web upload to the immich shared folder, in photos: the originals in photos/upload (or, once Immich's storage template is on, in photos/library, a folder for each person), with thumbnails and converted videos beside them. File Station shows them — include the whole immich shared folder in Hyper Backup.

Immich is working on

Machine learning lets Immich search by what's in a photo ("dog on a beach", "red car"), recognise faces and gather them into people, find duplicates and read the text in photos. It runs in a container of its own that needs 1–2 GB of memory while it works. On a Synology with 4 GB or less, turning it off keeps everything else quick — uploads, the timeline, albums, sharing and the map work just the same without it.

Locked out?

Resets the password of Immich's administrator with Immich's own immich-admin reset-admin-password. The new password is shown once, and everyone signed in as the administrator is signed out.

The Immich app backs up each phone's camera roll to this Synology — in the background, new photos only — and shows the whole library. Everyone signs in with their own Immich account.

Set up a phone

  1. Install Immich: App Store for iPhone and iPad, Google Play or F-Droid for Android.
  2. Open it and enter the server address:
  3. Sign in with your Immich email and password.
  4. Tap the cloud icon, pick the albums to back up, and turn backup on — and background backup, so it carries on by itself.
